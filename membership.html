Become a member

Membership is open throughout the year. Join the families keeping Telugu culture alive in Atlanta.

Why join

  • Most TAMA events are open to the public, and members enjoy member benefits at events.
  • Get the regular TAMA email newsletter with event news, movie screenings, and community classifieds.
  • Support Mana Badi, the Free Medical Clinic, and the festivals your family loves.
