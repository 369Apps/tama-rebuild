What we do

Festivals through the year

Sankranthi, Ugadi, Bathukamma, Dussehra, and Deepavali, celebrated with music, dance, food, and family.

For kids and families

KidsFest competitions, the Mana Badi weekend Telugu school, knowledge bowls, and a summer picnic.

Care for the community

A Free Medical Clinic for the uninsured, sports tournaments, movie screenings, and volunteer service.
